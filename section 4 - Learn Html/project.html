Event Description

Welcome to the premier web development event of TechFest 2026! Whether you are an aspiring creator looking to build your first website or a student eager to master modern industry tools, this hands-on workshop is designed to elevate your digital skills from concept to deployment.The digital world is evolving rapidly, and web development remains at the core of this transformation. This comprehensive session bridges the gap between foundational theory and real-world application, equipping you with the practical knowledge needed to architect the modern web.

Important Information

  • Prerequisites:No prior advanced coding experience is required! However, a basic familiarity with how to navigate a computer and a text editor is highly recommended.

  • Check-In Details: Reporting starts at 9:30 AM IST. Please bring a digital or printed copy of your registration QR code/ticket for seamless entry.
  • On-Campus Amenities:Complimentary Wi-Fi access will be provided at the venue. Complimentary lunch and refreshments will also be served during the mid-day break.
